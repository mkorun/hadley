punch — credits

The layout of punch follows two themes published under the MIT licence: the "background" home page of Blowfish
(a picture as wide as the screen with the person on it) and the post cards of Ghost's Casper. Neither's code is
copied: punch is written anew in its own templates and CSS. Their notices follow, as their licences ask.

The icons of the profiles are from Simple Icons (https://simpleicons.org), CC0 1.0; the LinkedIn mark is punch's own.
Fraunces, Inter and IBM Plex Mono are under the SIL Open Font License (fonts/OFL-Fraunces.txt, fonts/OFL-Inter.txt,
fonts/OFL-IBM-Plex-Mono.txt).
