# Reading a contact sheet

**Twenty frames on one page: what a contact sheet shows that a single picture hides.**

Before I choose a picture, I look at all of them at once, small, in the order I made them. A contact sheet shows the
walk, not just the destination: where I hesitated, where I came back, where the light changed and I did not notice.

The best frame is often the one just after the one I meant to take. The sheet makes that visible. A single picture,
printed large, never would.
