# Why I still print

**A picture on a screen is a promise; a print is the picture keeping it.**

On a screen a photograph is always a little provisional. It can be brighter, darker, larger, gone. A print has made
up its mind. It has a size, a paper, a weight in the hand, and it looks the same tomorrow.

So once a month I print the best of what I made, small, on matte paper, and pin them to the wall above my desk. The
ones that are still there a month later go on this site.
