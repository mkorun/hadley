# Light on walls

**Lisbon is a city of pale walls, and for an hour each evening they become a screen.**

The light here comes in low from the river. It catches on a balcony rail, a washing line, the edge of a roof, and
throws all of it across the house opposite. For a while every wall in the old town carries a drawing of the street
it stands in.

I started photographing these drawings on my way home and never quite stopped. The rule is simple: only the wall,
never the thing that casts the shadow. What you see is the light's version of the street, not mine.

## How

A small camera, a fixed lens, the hour before sunset. Most pictures are made in under a minute; the light moves too
fast for anything else.
