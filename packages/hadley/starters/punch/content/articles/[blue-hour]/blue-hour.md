# The blue hour

**Twenty minutes after sunset the city turns blue, and the lights come on one window at a time.**

Photographers call it the blue hour, though it rarely lasts an hour. The sky is still brighter than the streets, so
the houses stand dark against it, and every lamp that comes on is a small event.

I like it for the quiet as much as for the colour. The day's noise is over and the night's has not begun. For a few
minutes the city looks like a drawing of itself.
